## Market Overview

As of September 2026, the US data center pipeline contains **7,057 facilities**: 2,091 operational, 823 under construction, and 4,143 announced. The number of planned projects increased 203% between March and August 2026. Over 5.2 GW of new capacity is under construction, with another 8.1 GW in planning and permitting.

Construction costs average **$8.6M per MW** in Q1 2026 (up from $7.5M in 2025). Average permitted capacity per project is **110 MW**, up from ~70 MW in Q1 2025. In Q1 2026 alone, **2,192 MW** of data center capacity was permitted.

## Water Consumption Data

### Industry-Wide Numbers

- US data centers used an estimated **264 billion gallons** of water in 2025 (including electricity generation water)
- Annual direct consumption projected to reach **38–73 billion gallons** by 2028
- A medium data center uses ~**300,000 gallons/day**
- A hyperscale facility uses **1–5 million gallons/day**
- A standard medium facility (~110 MW) consumes ~**110 million gallons/year**, equivalent to a town of 50,000 residents
- Data centers are ~0.2% of US water use, but **~40% sit in high water-stress areas**
- More than **50 cities** have enacted moratoriums related to data center water use

### Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE) Benchmarks

| Cooling method | Typical WUE (L/kWh) | Water impact |

## Regulatory & Permit Landscape

### Federal

- USACE has issued **21 individual permits** for data centers between 2009 and 2026, with additional permits pending
- A court ruling shrinking the scope of the Clean Water Act has allowed data centers to speed through the federal permitting process
- CRS reports on data center water ([R49057](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R49057)) and energy infrastructure permits ([R48762](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48762)) are available

### State Legislation (2026)

Over **300 bills** targeting data center regulation filed across **30+ states** in 2026.

| State | Bill | Status | Key provision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West Virginia | HB 4832 | Proposed | Limits data center water usage if causing adverse supply impacts; requires data centers to pay all water utility costs |
| Illinois | SB 2181 | Proposed | Requires disclosure of water usage, public reporting, state analysis and mitigation recommendations |
| Massachusetts | Executive order (Healey) | Active | Proposed data centers subjected to local approval process |
| California | Multiple | Proposed | Require operators to submit regular water use reports |
| Iowa | Multiple | Proposed | Water use reporting requirements |
| Michigan | Multiple | Proposed | Water use reporting requirements |
| Maryland | SB 978 | Failed (2025) | Would have conditioned permits on no adverse environmental impact + community review |

### Key Permit Types to Track

| Permit type | Issuing authority | What it reveals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conditional Use Permit / Rezoning | County zoning board | Site location, facility size, project timeline |
| Environmental Assessment (EA/EIS) | State environmental agency | Modeled noise levels, water supply demands, traffic impact |
| NPDES Permit | EPA / state agency | Water runoff and discharge volumes |
| Air Quality Permit | State environmental agency | Diesel backup generator emissions, hours of operation |
| Water Authority Agreement | Local water utility | Guaranteed water draw, peak capacity, drought contingency |
| Building Permit | County/city | Construction cost, square footage, power infrastructure |
